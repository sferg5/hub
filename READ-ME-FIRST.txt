FABRIC LAB — drop-in page for your existing site
================================================

Three files, all named "holocloth" so they won't touch your existing index page
or its assets. No build step, no Node.

UPLOAD THESE THREE FILES (and nothing else):
    holocloth.html
    holocloth.js
    holocloth.css

Put all three together in the SAME folder in your site's repo. You can drop them
at the top level (next to your index.html) or inside a subfolder — either works,
because the paths are relative.

Your page will then be at:
    https://your-site/holocloth.html            (if at top level)
    https://your-site/<subfolder>/holocloth.html (if in a subfolder)

Link to it from your existing index page however you like.

(Do NOT upload this READ-ME-FIRST.txt.)

To change fabrics or tune look/drape, edit src/fabrics.ts in the source project
(fabric-lab-source.zip) and rebuild — the build regenerates these three files.
