FABRIC LAB — drop-in page for your existing site
================================================
Upload these three files (same folder), nothing else:
    holocloth.html
    holocloth.js
    holocloth.css
They won't touch your existing index page. Link to holocloth.html from your site.
Pick a fabric from the "Fabric" dropdown (top-right). Drag on the cloth to pull it;
hold Space + drag to orbit.
