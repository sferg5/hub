FABRIC FINDER — drop-in page for your existing site
===================================================
Upload these files (same folder), nothing else:
    holocloth.html
    holocloth.js
They won't touch your existing index page. Link to holocloth.html from your site.

The page: an intro, a 4-question "find my fabric" quiz, a fabric library, and a
detail view with an interactive 3D fabric preview, care info, activities and
products (with prices, linking to lululemon).

To update products/prices or the quiz, edit src/content.ts in the source project
(fabric-finder-source.zip) and rebuild.
