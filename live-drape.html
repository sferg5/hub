Live Drape — Hoodie

A simulated hoodie — body, sleeves and hood — skinned to your pose in real time. Stand back so your shoulders and arms are in frame, then move: raise your arms, turn, lean. The garment wraps and follows.

Everything runs locally on your device; nothing is uploaded. If the camera doesn’t start when opening the file directly, serve the folder — e.g. python3 -m http.server — and open http://localhost:8000/live-drape.html.
