Five minutes of still

A ring that breathes, sound that breathes with it, and a camera that quietly listens to your heart through the light on your skin. Eyes open, you’ll see your heart and your breath. Close your eyes, and the room dissolves into light — it keeps listening. When it’s over, you’ll see what your heart did.

Sit comfortably with soft, even light on your face. Headphones are lovely but not required. If the camera doesn’t start when opening the file directly, serve the folder — python3 -m http.server → http://localhost:8000/live-still.html. Nothing is uploaded.
