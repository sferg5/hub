Kaleidoscope Visualizer

Full-frame neon kaleidoscope driven by your hands and face, tracked in your browser. Right hand shapes the pattern (spin, zoom, symmetry, line width). Head turn morphs the shape and tilt sets its color. Left hand paints the smooth pastel background.

Right hand drives the pattern, left the background; open a hand for full opacity, close it to fade that layer. Gestures: bump fists → explosion · touch index fingertips → flare · tap your head (right hand = foreground pattern, left hand = background pattern) · pinch one hand → that layer pulses; rotate the hand like a knob to speed it up · pinch both hands and move them apart or together → scrub how kaleidoscopey the pattern is; let go to leave it there 🌀. Head tilt sets both layers' colors as a complementary pair.

Best in Chrome. You'll be asked for camera permission.
