The screen is a window

Behind the glass there’s a forest, and it’s alive. Your camera watches where your eyes are, and the perspective re-projects in real time — lean around the tree at the edge of the frame. The breeze and birdsong are synthesized live, the gusts you hear move the branches you see… and echoes of you, moments old, stand among the trees.

Works best at arm’s length from the screen. If the camera doesn’t start when opening the file directly, serve the folder — python3 -m http.server → http://localhost:8000/live-window.html. Nothing is uploaded.
